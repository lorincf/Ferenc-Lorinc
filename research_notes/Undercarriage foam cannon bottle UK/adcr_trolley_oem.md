## 1. What the ADCR listing says about the inline foam cannon and bottle

### Takeaway
ADCR sells a 16" (40cm) trolley with an "inline foam cannon", 4 black soap nozzles and 4 white 40° rinse nozzles, rated at 4000 PSI, for £84.99. In the snippets I found, the listing says nothing about the bottle's capacity or thread, or about whether the bottle or cannon can be removed.

### Cited Findings
- Title: "1/4\" Quick Release Foam Cannon Washer Undercarriage Chassis Cleaner". Price £84.99. Width 16 in (40cm). "4x 1/4in quick release connections for nozzles (4x black soap nozzles and 4x white 40° rinse nozzles included)". Max pressure 4000 PSI. Includes an inline foam cannon and extension bars. — [ADCR product page (via search snippet)](https://adcrdetailing.co.uk/products/1-4-quick-release-foam-washer-premium-undercarriage-chassis-car-cleaner)
- ADCR's pitch, in paraphrase: most foam-cannon undercarriage cleaners have fixed soap nozzles, so they can't pressure-rinse properly afterwards. Standard undercarriage cleaners have high-pressure rinse nozzles that "don't allow soap to be drawn in" from an inline cannon. ADCR's version lets you foam with the black soap nozzles and then swap to the white 40° nozzles to rinse, with no extra accessories. The listing calls this a "UK first". — [ADCR product page (via search snippet)](https://adcrdetailing.co.uk/products/1-4-quick-release-foam-washer-premium-undercarriage-chassis-car-cleaner)
- One search summary said the product was listed as **sold out**. — [ADCR product page (via search snippet)](https://adcrdetailing.co.uk/products/1-4-quick-release-foam-washer-premium-undercarriage-chassis-car-cleaner)
- The same collection has a version without the foam cannon at £43.99. ADCR also sells 13in and 16in "Quick Release" undercarriage cleaners with 4 high-pressure nozzles. — [ADCR undercarriage collection](https://adcrdetailing.co.uk/collections/undercarriage-car-chassis-cleaners); [ADCR 16in cleaner](https://adcrdetailing.co.uk/products/quick-release-undercarriage-cleaner-16in); [ADCR premium 16in](https://adcrdetailing.co.uk/products/quick-release-premium-undercarriage-chassis-car-cleaner-16in-40cm)
- A general nozzle guide says black soap nozzles, often 65°, are low-pressure soap tips with a large orifice, and white 40° nozzles are wide-angle rinse tips. This supports the swap logic: the large-orifice black tips drop the pressure enough for the inline venturi to draw soap. — [nozzle-pro.com](https://nozzle-pro.com/blogs/blog/optimizing-your-car-wash-with-the-right-nozzles)

### Inferences
- "Inline" here means the foam cannon sits **between the gun and the bar/trolley**: 1/4" QC male plug in, 1/4" QC female socket out. It is not a cannon that sprays its own foam. Foam comes out through the trolley's nozzles, and only when the large-orifice soap tips are fitted.
- Because the cannon sits on the 1/4" QC line, it is almost certainly a separate, detachable unit (gun → cannon → extension bar → trolley), not a bottle built into the trolley deck. I could not confirm this from photos.

### Gaps
- Exact ADCR wording on bottle capacity, bottle thread, and whether the cannon head detaches. I could not read the page or its images.
- No ADCR eBay listing for this product turned up in search (tried eBay.co.uk queries).

## 2. Is it the same OEM product as Tool Daily, Twinkle Star, RIDGE WASHER and M MINGLE 16" kits?

### Takeaway
The likely answer is yes in concept, but I could not prove an identical part. The Tool Daily, Twinkle Star and M MINGLE 16" "with foam cannon" kits all use the same arrangement: a 1L foam cannon fitted inline between the gun and the wand, with **large-orifice brass nozzles** on the trolley for foaming and stainless high-pressure nozzles for rinsing. That is the same principle as ADCR's black/white nozzle swap, so ADCR's "UK first" claim is marketing. I could not match photos, so it is unconfirmed whether ADCR uses the exact same OEM trolley and cannon. The closest match by spec count is the Amazon "Ryansunoro" 16" kit with 8 nozzles and 3 wands.

### Cited Findings
- The Tool Daily foam cannon plus dual-function undercarriage cleaner listing says: "When using the foam cannon, the nozzles on the under carriage cleaner are the brass nozzles, otherwise the stainless steel nozzles will cause leakage at the connection between the foam cannon and wand". To run without the cannon "you have to change the four nozzles to stainless steel ones". — [Amazon, Tool Daily B09QKQSB71 (search summary)](https://www.amazon.com/clp/B09QKQSB71)
- Tool Daily 16" undercarriage cleaner kit: comes with a 1 Liter foam cannon, and "All parts have 1/4\" Quick Connect". One listing shows 3 extension wands and 3600 PSI; others say 4000 PSI. — [Amazon, Tool Daily B0DGX65SLX](https://www.amazon.com/Tool-Daily-Undercarriage-Attachment-Dual-Function/dp/B0DGX65SLX); [eBay, Tool Daily 16"](https://www.ebay.com/itm/376012573855)

The foam blaster is a 1-litre bottle with a 1/4" QC and "can be used with a black nozzle and wands" for washing the car's surface. Rated 1500–4000 PSI, 2500+ recommended. — [Amazon.co.uk M MINGLE](https://www.amazon.co.uk/MINGLE-Undercarriage-Pressure-Attachment-Powerful/dp/B0FR4VQ3Y9); [Amazon.com M MINGLE](https://www.amazon.com/MINGLE-Undercarriage-Pressure-Attachment-Powerful/dp/B0FR4VQ3Y9)

### Inferences
- "Brass large-aperture nozzles" (Tool Daily, Twinkle Star, M MINGLE) and "black soap nozzles" (ADCR) do the same job. All these kits are the same inline-cannon design, so the trolley plugs into a female 1/4" QC outlet on the cannon (or on its wand).

Note that Ryansunoro calls its bottle "built in", which could mean the bottle is on the trolley rather than inline at the gun. That conflicts with the ADCR inference and is unresolved.

## 3. How the foam cannon mounts, and the bottle thread

### Takeaway
On these kits the foam cannon connects by 1/4" QC and sits inline between the gun and the wand. In the kits that describe it this way (Tool Daily, M MINGLE), the cannon is removable from the trolley chain. **I found no source that states the bottle neck thread for any of these undercarriage-kit cannons.** On generic 1L foam cannons the bottle unscrews from the brass head, but I found no stated thread spec.

### Cited Findings
- Tool Daily's leak warning refers to "the connection between the foam cannon and wand", which shows the cannon sits upstream of the wand and trolley. — [Amazon B09QKQSB71 (search summary)](https://www.amazon.com/clp/B09QKQSB71)
- Typical standalone 1L foam cannons have a 1/4" QC male plug and are rated about 1000–3000 PSI. Some, such as Forney's 34oz, are rated 3700 PSI at 2.0–5.3 GPM. — [Forney](https://www.forneyind.com/Foam-Cannon-34-Oz-Quick-Connect-1-4-in-Plug); [PWAccs](https://www.pwaccs.com/products/pressure-washer-foam-cannon-5-colored-spray-nozzle-tips-quick-connect)
- Several 1L foam blasters state "Requires Pressure Washer to Have 2.0GPM & Over 1000 PSI". This is relevant to the Active 2.0 at 2.0 GPM and about 1100 psi. — [Amazon, generic 1L 1/4" foam cannon B0F3HZGTMV](https://www.amazon.com/clp/B0F3HZGTMV)
- The undercarriage kits recommend 2500 PSI or more (Twinkle Star, M MINGLE, RIDGE WASHER), and one Twinkle Star listing asks for at least 2.5 GPM. The Active 2.0 is below both. — [Amazon.co.uk M MINGLE](https://www.amazon.co.uk/MINGLE-Undercarriage-Pressure-Attachment-Powerful/dp/B0FR4VQ3Y9); [Amazon Twinkle Star](https://www.amazon.com/Twinkle-Star-Dual-Function-Undercarriage-Cleaner/dp/B08V5K7XCZ)
- UK snow-foam-lance bottle threads: a Detailing World user found "most if not all lances are 28mm thread", with Autobrite at 27mm, and noted that spare-bottle listings usually don't state the thread. The Dr Dirt 0.8L replacement bottle lists a "Thread diameter 28 mm". — [Detailing World thread](https://www.detailingworld.co.uk/threads/foam-lance-bottle-thread-size.379210/); [High Definition Detail, Dr Dirt bottle](https://www.highdefinitiondetail.co.uk/products/dr-dirt-replacement-foam-lance-bottle-0-8-litre-upgraded-snow-foam-lance-bottle)
- The "M22" figure that appears on foam-cannon listings refers to the gun-side connection (for example, "22mm male thread, inner hole diameter 14mm"), **not** the bottle neck. — [Amazon.co.uk Stone Banks lance](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Adjustable-Sprayer-Dispenser-Ubermann-Pressure/dp/B07V5HTRBH); [Amazon Tool Daily bottle](https://www.amazon.com/Tool-Daily-Cannon-Bottle-Replacement/dp/B08KXR21M4)

### Inferences
- If ADCR's cannon follows this family, it can be unplugged from the bar (1/4" QC) and the bottle unscrews from the brass head. The bottle neck is probably in the ~28mm class used by most Chinese 1L lances, but this is **unverified**. The user should measure the outside diameter of the neck thread with calipers, or ask ADCR.
- Practical fallback: if the bottle thread can't be matched, a complete generic 1L 1/4" QC foam cannon would do the same job, but only if the replacement also has a **female 1/4" QC outlet** (inline pass-through). Most standalone cannons have only a spray nozzle at the front and no QC outlet, so they would not feed the trolley.

### Gaps
- Bottle neck thread for the ADCR, Tool Daily, Twinkle Star or M MINGLE kit cannons: no source found.
- Whether ADCR's bottle is a transparent "wide neck" or a narrow-neck HDPE type: unknown.

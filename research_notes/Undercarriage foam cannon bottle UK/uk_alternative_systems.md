## 2. Is there an undercarriage trolley where a standard 1/4" QC foam cannon (for example MJJC S V3) can be plugged in upstream inline, i.e. a foam cannon with a 1/4" female outlet?

### Takeaway
Yes. A category of "dual-connector" or pass-through foam cannons exists. These take 1/4" QC male in from the gun and give a 1/4" female QC outlet that an undercarriage trolley (or nozzle) plugs into, usually with a shuttle valve to switch between foam and rinse. Tool Daily and RIDGE WASHER sell these, and dual-connector accessories appear on amazon.co.uk at roughly £12 to £50. The MJJC S V3 itself is a standard terminal cannon with no pass-through outlet, and a spare bottle for the S V3 specifically was not found in the UK.

### Cited Findings
- Tool Daily Pressure Washer Foam Cannon with Dual-Connector Accessory (double-tip attachment, 1/4" QC, 5 nozzles). — [Amazon.com B0CCJ9DJ4X](https://www.amazon.com/Tool-Daily-Dual-Connector-Accessory-Attachment/dp/B0CCJ9DJ4X)
- RIDGE WASHER Pressure Washer Gun Foam Cannon with Dual Connector Accessory, marketed alongside its undercarriage cleaner. Listed max flow is 8 GPM. — [Amazon.com B0FC655TFC](https://www.amazon.com/RIDGE-WASHER-Connector-Accessory-Undercarriage/dp/B0FC655TFC)

### Inferences
- The most robust fallback is: gun (3/8" F) → 3/8"-to-1/4" adapter → dual-connector or pass-through foam cannon with a 1/4" female outlet → any 1/4" QC wheeled undercarriage cleaner (including the ADCR trolley with its own foam bottle removed or bypassed, or a Clean and Shiny / High Definition Detail / generic eBay unit). The foam bottle is then the cannon's own bottle. Tool Daily's generic 1 L bottle and other "universal" 1 L bottles are widely sold.
- With a dual-connector valve, the foam cannon sits on one branch and the trolley on the other, so the trolley does not receive foam through the cannon. To foam through the trolley, you need a true pass-through cannon with a 1/4" female outlet, or you use the ADCR-style integrated design. Check the listing photos and Q&A for which design each product uses.
- The MJJC S V3 is not a pass-through design. It can be used as a separate foaming step before the trolley, but not inline.

### Gaps
- Could not verify from UK listings that any dual-connector cannon outputs foam to the trolley branch, as opposed to switching between branches.
- MJJC S V3 spare-bottle availability in the UK is unconfirmed. The claim that in2Detailing and My Car Cleaning sell S V3 spare bottles was not corroborated by search.

## 3. Minimum flow requirements vs Active 2.0 (2.0 GPM / 7.6 L/min, ~1100 psi working): flag anything needing more than 2.0 GPM

### Takeaway
Most listings give maximum ratings (4000 PSI, 5.3 to 8 GPM max flow) rather than minimums. The minimums that were found point to trouble at about 1100 psi and 2.0 GPM. One dual-function undercarriage bundle states a 2.5 GPM and over 1000 PSI minimum, Tool Daily and RIDGE WASHER say performance is best at 2500 PSI or more, and one dual-connector kit says 3000 PSI minimum. Expect weak rinse and foam on an Active 2.0. Check each listing's Q&A before buying.

### Cited Findings
- One dual-function undercarriage cleaner bundle requires a pressure washer with 2.5 GPM and over 1000 PSI. This is above the user's 2.0 GPM. A similar listing quoted 6 GPM and max 3000 PSI (this looks like an error or a different product). The specific product behind each figure was not clear from the snippet. — [search across Tool Daily / RIDGE WASHER / Twinkle Star listings](https://www.amazon.com/Twinkle-Star-Dual-Function-Undercarriage-Cleaner/dp/B08V5K7XCZ)
- Tool Daily undercarriage: working range 1500 to 4000 PSI, better at 2500 PSI or more. The user's roughly 1100 psi working pressure is below the stated range. — [shopabunda Tool Daily listing](https://www.shopabunda.com/products/tool-daily-undercarriage-pressure-washer-attachment-16-inch-dual-function-undercarriage-cleaner-for-power-washer-and-garden-hose-under-car-wash-water-broom-with-3-pieces-extension-wand-4000-psi)
- RIDGE WASHER undercarriage: rated 1500 to 4000 PSI, optimal at 2500 PSI or more. — [shopabunda RIDGE listing](https://www.shopabunda.com/products/ridge-washer-pressure-washer-undercarriage-cleaner-16-inch-undercarriage-washer-pressure-washer-under-car-cleaner-with-straight-extension-wand-4000-psi)
- Tool Daily standard foam cannon: compatible with 1000 to 3000 PSI washers, so 1100 psi is in range. — [woodartsupply mirror](https://woodartsupply.com/a/s/products/tool-daily-foam-cannon-with-1-4-inch-quick-connector-1-liter-5-pressure-washer-nozzle-tips)
- One dual-connector kit on Amazon UK states "Minimum PSI required: 3000". — [Amazon.co.uk search](https://www.amazon.co.uk/foam-cannon-dual-connector-accessory/s?k=foam+cannon+dual+connector+accessory)
- Pro-Kleen chassis cleaner kits are designed around UK consumer washers (Lavor, Nilfisk, Bosch, Kärcher K), rated up to 220 bar. No minimum flow is stated. — [Pro-Kleen](https://www.prokleen.co.uk/pro-kleen-chassis-cleaner-with-lavor-snow-foam-lance-1)

### Inferences
- **FLAG:** the 16" four-nozzle trolley bundles (Tool Daily, RIDGE WASHER, Twinkle Star and similar) are aimed at 2.5 GPM-class gas washers. At 2.0 GPM and about 1100 psi, the four 1/4" nozzles will split a small flow. Fitting smaller-orifice or narrower-angle nozzles, or a unit whose nozzles are swappable 1/4" QC tips (as ADCR's are), helps.
- Foam cannons on a 2.0 GPM electric washer usually need a 1.1 mm or 1.25 mm orifice for good foam. Whether these bundled cannons ship with a small orifice is unknown.
- Pro-Kleen kits, built for UK electric washers, are probably a better match for a 2.0 GPM machine, but no minimum flow is published.

### Gaps
- No product found publishes a minimum GPM at or below 2.0 GPM.
- Orifice sizes for the bundled foam cannons were not found.

# Buy the Tool Daily cannon, swap its bottle

**My top pick is the Tool Daily 1L foam cannon with a 1/4" quick connect, Amazon.co.uk ASIN B094F865YP, at about £23.99 and listed in stock** ([Amazon.co.uk](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Tool-Daily-Connect-Pressure-Nozzles/dp/B094F865YP)). You unscrew its 1L bottle and use it as the second bottle on the ADCR trolley's inline head. Tool Daily is one of the Chinese makers that sells the same inline-cannon undercarriage design as ADCR, so its bottle is the closest brand-family match you can buy in the UK today. The spare cannon head is also useful on its own for pre-washing bodywork. **The backup is the Pro-Kleen 1L spare bottle at £11.15**, listed in stock ([Pro-Kleen](https://www.prokleen.co.uk/spare-bottle-for-the-pro-kleen-snow-foam-lance)). Nobody publishes the neck thread for the ADCR inline cannon, and no UK bottle says it fits undercarriage cleaners. So spend two minutes with calipers before you order: a neck of about 28mm means either pick will almost certainly fit. Separately, the Active 2.0's 2.0 GPM at about 1100 psi is below what these 16" foam trolleys are designed for. Expect thinner foam and a weaker rinse whichever bottle you use.

You foam through the black tips, then swap to the white ones to rinse ([ADCR](https://adcrdetailing.co.uk/products/1-4-quick-release-foam-washer-premium-undercarriage-chassis-car-cleaner)). Swapping between pre-wash and Atom-Mac is then: unscrew one bottle, screw on the other, and flush the head briefly with water between products.

## 28mm is the number to check

The best evidence points to **a 28mm neck (28/400 or 28-410) as the near-universal thread on generic brass-knob foam cannons**. UK detailers on DetailingWorld report that "most if not all lances are 28mm", with an older Autobrite lance at 27mm ([DetailingWorld](https://www.detailingworld.co.uk/threads/foam-lance-bottle-thread-size.379210/)). The MTM standard bottle is specified as 28-410 ([Auto Obsessed](https://www.autoobsessed.com/products/foam-cannon-bottle-replacement)), and the Dr Dirt spare states a 28mm thread ([High Definition Detail](https://www.highdefinitiondetail.co.uk/products/dr-dirt-replacement-foam-lance-bottle-0-8-litre-upgraded-snow-foam-lance-bottle)). The same forum thread shows that a bottle sold as "28mm" can come out 0.5–1mm oversize, so a label alone doesn't guarantee a fit. The "M22" figure on foam-cannon listings refers to the gun connection, not the bottle neck ([Amazon.co.uk](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Adjustable-Sprayer-Dispenser-Ubermann-Pressure/dp/B07V5HTRBH)).

To verify, unscrew the ADCR bottle and measure the **outside diameter across the thread crests** with calipers. **A reading of about 27.5–28.5mm means buy the Tool Daily cannon or the Pro-Kleen bottle with confidence.** If it is clearly larger, around 38mm or more, you have a wide-mouth bottle: skip both picks and email ADCR for a matching spare. Also avoid wide-mouth bottles (MTM PF22.2, which needs an adapter on older cannons) and MJJC Pro bayonet bottles, because neither uses the generic thread ([Clean Garage](https://clean-garage.com/mtm-wide-mouth-foam-cannon-bottle-pf22-2-bottle-only-no-adapter/); [CA Detailing](https://www.cadetailing.co.uk/collections/mjjc)). One handling note: buyers report that these plastic threads wear fast when over-tightened into a brass head, so go hand-tight only, especially if you'll swap bottles several times a day ([eBay UK](https://www.ebay.co.uk/itm/256103624071)).

## Your 2.0 GPM washer is the weak link

The bottle choice doesn't change pressure, and that is where the real risk sits. **Tool Daily and RIDGE WASHER rate their 16" foam trolleys at 1500–4000 PSI, with best results at 2500 PSI or more**, and one dual-function bundle asks for at least 2.5 GPM ([shopabunda, Tool Daily](https://www.shopabunda.com/products/tool-daily-undercarriage-pressure-washer-attachment-16-inch-dual-function-undercarriage-cleaner-for-power-washer-and-garden-hose-under-car-wash-water-broom-with-3-pieces-extension-wand-4000-psi); [shopabunda, RIDGE WASHER](https://www.shopabunda.com/products/ridge-washer-pressure-washer-undercarriage-cleaner-16-inch-undercarriage-washer-pressure-washer-under-car-cleaner-with-straight-extension-wand-4000-psi); [Amazon, Twinkle Star](https://www.amazon.com/Twinkle-Star-Dual-Function-Undercarriage-Cleaner/dp/B08V5K7XCZ)). The Active 2.0 runs at about 1100 psi and splits 2.0 GPM across four nozzles. Expect wet, runny foam under the car and a gentler rinse than the marketing shows. The cannon itself is less of a problem: Tool Daily rates its standalone cannon for 1000–3000 PSI, so 1100 psi is in range ([Tool Daily listing mirror](https://woodartsupply.com/a/s/products/tool-daily-foam-cannon-with-1-4-inch-quick-connect-1-liter-5-pressure-washer-nozzle-tips)). If foam is weak, Tool Daily's 1.1mm orifice and mesh kit (B088JV5GF5) suits low-flow electric washers ([Amazon.co.uk](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Tool-Daily-Cannon-Orifice-Nozzle/dp/B088JV5GF5)). Also skip "dual-connector" shuttle-valve kits as a workaround: at least one UK listing requires 3000 PSI minimum ([Amazon.co.uk search](https://www.amazon.co.uk/foam-cannon-dual-connector-accessory/s?k=foam+cannon+dual+connector+accessory)). Finally, the gun's 3/8" female outlet needs a 3/8" male to 1/4" female adapter in front of the cannon.

## Conclusion

The fix is a bottle, not a new system. Every 16" foam trolley sold in the UK and US uses the same generic brass head, so the problem comes down to one measurement: if the neck is about 28mm, the Tool Daily cannon gives you a second bottle today plus a spare cannon. The one thing still unproven is the ADCR head's actual thread, and nobody publishes it. Your calipers settle that, or one email to ADCR. If foam under the car still disappoints after the swap, the cause is the 1100 psi washer, not the bottle. The next upgrade to consider is a smaller orifice, or eventually a higher-flow machine, not more bottles.
